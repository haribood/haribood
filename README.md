## About me


I’m an electrical engineering student at the **University of Houston**, working across **embedded systems, robotics, and controls**. I enjoy connecting a physical build with the firmware and feedback that bring it to life.


- Building robots with sensor feedback, maze mapping, and closed-loop motion control.
- Research experience in magnetic microswimmer actuation, PID control, and dual-camera tracking.
- Hands-on circuit assembly, discrete-transistor logic, and high-voltage hardware projects.
- Seeking a **2027 engineering internship**.


## Tools and technologies


**Languages:** C, C++  
**Hardware:** ESP32 / ESP32-S3, IR sensors, encoders, IMU feedback, motor drivers, ESP-NOW  
**Software and methods:** LabVIEW, OpenCV, PID, closed-loop control, computer vision


**Research:** Magnetic actuation, PID control, and computer vision
